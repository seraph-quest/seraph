# Historical Agent-Parity Research Program

**State:** Archived

This page summarizes the research program associated with closed issue
[#475](https://github.com/seraph-quest/seraph/issues/475). It compared public
Hermes, OpenClaw, and IronClaw capabilities and maintained bounded claim rules.
Its detailed goals, phase instructions, benchmark tables, and source snapshot
remain available in Git history.

The material is no longer a product target, active research agenda, or execution
queue. The [Project Constitution](/) is the sole accepted-target authority;
[Development Status](/status) owns shipped truth. Current comparative claims,
when needed, require fresh primary sources and the
[Strategy Claim Ledger](./19-strategy-claim-ledger.md).
