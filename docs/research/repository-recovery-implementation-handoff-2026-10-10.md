## Observable result and boundaries

A NEW v4 original-producer admission can recover its authentic already-completed bounded work after backend loss, including a rollback followed by normal authentication touch or same-session bearer refresh. Recovery reuses the original Root, Task/Attempt, producer, accounting group, fences and cutoffs. It cannot dispatch missing commands, replay ambiguous effects, mint a new lease, renew approval/budget/deadline or rebind a new login. Existing public recovery remains fail-closed 503 until the complete authenticated API/UI and same-database managed kill/restart acceptance is proved.

Historical v1/v2/v3 registrations and artifacts remain historical. Never rewrite an inventory, retro-register a producer, reconstruct an immutable commitment from current rows or reinterpret old mixed audit digests. Existing unsupported debt remains visibly held; it does not become success or free capacity.

## Fresh authorization is always separate

Every retry reauthenticates the exact original active session and checks current bearer validity, idle and absolute expiry, revocation, tombstone and replacement state. In-place real refresh may rotate `token_hash` and activity timestamps but preserves the original session/principal/absolute expiry. A new login, including one sharing a data-continuity identity, never rebinds execution. `authenticate_websocket_session` replacement-following is forbidden here.

Stage complete CURRENT context rows, proposal/approval, original group and complete reservation vector; then compare those full bytes exactly to fresh IMMEDIATE-writer rows under the same Source/configuration fence. Auth changes between staging and CAS cause bounded restaging/denial, not a weakened SQL predicate. Keep Goal/native mapping, artifact TTL, source/profile/runtime, original budget/cutoff, held/Unknown liability, reservation membership/status/cost and fence checks. Immutable historical proof never substitutes for fresh permission. Recovery does not refresh or renew these limits.
