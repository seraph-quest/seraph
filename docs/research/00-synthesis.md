# Seraph Research Index

**State:** Research

The [Project Constitution](/) is the sole product and accepted-target authority.
This tree contains Research evidence, alternatives, dated comparisons, and historical
analysis. Nothing here establishes that a capability is accepted or shipped.

## How Research Becomes A Decision

1. Record evidence, sources, dates, limitations, and uncertainty here.
2. Open or refine a tracked issue for the proposed decision.
3. Use an independent Critic/Contrarian pass for architecture, security, memory,
   or competitive conclusions.
4. Accept a direction only by updating the constitution and adding or
   superseding an ADR.
5. Mark a capability Shipped only in implementation docs after merge and proof.
