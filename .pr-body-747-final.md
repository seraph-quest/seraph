## Problem

Adopted local capability effects could be replayed or journaled without a server-held secret, package/VCS commands could reach network-capable subcommands, and process/file output was not bounded before collection. Native software-engineering callers also needed a server-owned raw-result seam.

## Result

This PR completes the governed local capability boundary for the adopted filesystem and process tools. Effect identities and journal records use server-secret-derived MACs and fail closed when the secret is unavailable or wrong. Git/npm/uv network-capable operations and destinations are rejected, output and file reads are bounded before decoding, and native SWE obtains raw adapter results only through a module-private host path. Existing owner, capability, approval, destination, fence, restart-uncertainty, CPU/address-space/PID/process-group, and audit controls remain in force.
