## Problem

Web, WebSocket, observer, and approval paths could describe the same user interaction with different session identities. Restarted delivery and approval replay therefore had no canonical cross-surface lineage.

## Result

This milestone introduces a canonical conversation identity and signed, redacted envelope shared by web, WebSocket, observer, session, approval, and native-notification paths. Durable notification outbox records retain lineage, delivery fences, retry and cancellation state, and restart reconciliation without making provider calls.

## Scope

- derive and validate canonical conversation/session identity at ingress;
- carry owner, session, thread, request, and attachment lineage through chat, WebSocket, observer, and approval flows;
- redact attachment metadata and persist only bounded, integrity-checked payloads;
- make approvals owner-scoped and replay-safe;
- persist native notification intents and delivery attempts with bounded leases, fences, idempotency, retry, cancellation, and explicit unknown recovery;
- document the continuity contract.
