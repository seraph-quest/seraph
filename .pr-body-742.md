## What this changes

Completes the canonical workspace migration and recovery safeguards. Backup and restore now bind every operation to device/inode/path identity, coordinate through a stable parent-side owner lock, validate archives fail-closed, stage before promotion, and preserve security-sensitive state through rollback.

## Scope

- Harden `backup`, `restore`, `status`, `identity`, and `rollback` managed workspace paths.
- Reject root replacement, symlink/traversal/special-file/case-collision archives, and competing owners.
- Keep archive writes atomic and journals/checksums/redacted receipts durable.
- Reconcile tombstones, revocations, config history, sessions, workflow authority and cost liabilities during recovery.
- Exercise an isolated backup → mutation → restore → restart → rollback drill.
